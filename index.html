Mission Brief

Key questions about OMNIA AI

What makes OMNIA AI different?

We are a software-first company focused on creating a hardware-agnostic "intelligence layer" that retrofits existing drone fleets. Our models are trained on real-world data from the front lines, creating a powerful data moat.

How does it function when GPS is jammed?

OMNIA AI uses Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO). It fuses data from standard cameras and onboard IMUs to create a highly accurate, real-time position estimate, allowing it to navigate without any external signals.

Who is behind it?

Our team is composed of leading engineers, researchers, and strategists from UC Berkeley, with deep expertise in robotics, AI, and national security.
