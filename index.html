FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What hardware is compatible with OMNIA AI?

Our software is designed to be hardware-agnostic and can be integrated with a wide range of commercial and custom drone platforms. Contact us for specific integration requirements.

How does it perform in complete darkness or fog?

OMNIA AI leverages a multi-sensor fusion approach. While visual navigation is primary, it complements it with LiDAR and other sensors to maintain operational effectiveness in low-light and adverse weather conditions.

Is this a software or a hardware solution?

OMNIA AI is a pure software solution—an "intelligence layer" that enhances the capabilities of existing hardware. We do not manufacture drones.
