Mission Brief

Key questions about OMNIA AI

What makes OMNIA AI different?

We are a software-first company focused on creating a hardware-agnostic "intelligence layer" that retrofits existing drone fleets. Our models are trained on real-world data from the front lines, creating a powerful data moat.

How does it function when GPS is jammed?

OMNIA AI uses Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO). It fuses data from standard cameras and onboard IMUs to create a highly accurate, real-time position estimate, allowing it to navigate without any external signals.

Who is behind it?

Our team is composed of leading engineers, researchers, and strategists from UC Berkeley, with deep expertise in robotics, AI, and national security.

Where has it been tested?

Our systems are actively tested and validated in real-world conditions. We maintain a continuous feedback loop with operators and advisors on the front lines in Ukraine, ensuring our technology is hardened for missions in active GPS-jamming environments.

OMNIA AI: The Future of Autonomous Resilience

At its core, OMNIA AI is a vision-based autonomy system that empowers drones to operate in the most challenging environments imaginable—specifically, where GPS and traditional communication signals are absent or actively jammed.

Born from the innovative spirit of Silicon Valley and honed by insights from defense innovation roots, we've developed a software-first, hardware-agnostic intelligence layer. This means our technology can be seamlessly integrated into existing drone fleets, upgrading them with unprecedented independence.

We are building not just a product, but a new standard for uninterrupted mission capability. OMNIA AI ensures that when the signals die, autonomy doesn't just survive—it thrives.
